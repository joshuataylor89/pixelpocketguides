Top 5 Retro Handhelds with Dual Joysticks for Holiday 2026

If you want to play PSP, Dreamcast, GameCube, PS2 or Nintendo 64 games, you want two analog sticks. These are our top 5 retro handhelds with dual joysticks, from a budget Android pick to a Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 powerhouse, all sold on Amazon.

  • AYANEO Pocket Air Mini (From about $110)
  • Retroid Pocket Nova (~$229)

  • Anbernic RG477M (Around $240–$290)

Why dual joysticks matter

Many 3D games, from N64 to PS2 and GameCube, expect two analog sticks, one to move and one to aim the camera. A handheld with two sticks lets you play them the way they were designed. All five picks use Hall-effect sticks, which are designed to resist drift.
