What to look for in a dual-screen handheld

Check the size and shape of both screens, because the bottom screen is usually smaller and 4:3. Then look at the chip and RAM, which set how demanding a game you can run. Our database lists 3DS and NDS as the best-for systems for the Thor, the Duo and the Pocket DS. Weight matters too, since the range here runs from 321g to 540g.
