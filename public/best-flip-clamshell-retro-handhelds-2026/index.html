Top 5 Flip (Clamshell) Retro Handhelds for Holiday 2026

Updated November 10, 2026

A flip handheld folds shut to protect the screen and slips into a pocket, the same idea as the Game Boy Advance SP. Here are our top 5 flip (clamshell) retro handhelds on Amazon, from tiny budget pocket pals to a big-screen Android flip.

At a glance

  • Anbernic RG-34XXSP (~$67)
  • Miyoo Flip (~$78)
  • Retroid Pocket Flip 2 (~$190 to ~$220)
  • Anbernic RG SP (~$65)
  • Miyoo Mini Flip (~$60)

Why choose a flip handheld

The hinged lid protects the screen in a bag or pocket, and the shape feels like the classic Game Boy Advance SP. Most flip handhelds are small and affordable. The Retroid Pocket Flip 2 adds a big AMOLED screen and Android power.
