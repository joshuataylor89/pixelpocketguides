JOYSTICK-FREE HANDHELDS

Top 5 Joystick-Free Retro Handhelds for Holiday 2026

Updated November 11, 2026

No joystick, no problem. Classic 8-bit, 16-bit and Game Boy games were built for a D-pad, and handhelds without sticks stay small, light and cheap. Here are our top 5 joystick-free retro handhelds, all sold on Amazon.

At a glance

  • TRIMUI Brick (Around $70)
  • Anbernic RG35XX Plus (Around $50–$70)
  • Anbernic RG35XXSP (Around $65)
  • Analogue Pocket (Around $220–$240)
  • Anbernic RG Rotate (~$85)

Why choose a handheld without sticks

A D-pad-only handheld is smaller, lighter and usually cheaper. It is the best fit for Game Boy, GBA, NES, SNES and arcade games. If you also want N64, PSP or Dreamcast, see our dual-joystick picks.

Our ranked picks

#1

TRIMUI BrickBest Pocket Carry

Around $70 · $50–$100 tier

A tiny, well-designed brick that plays the 8-, 16- and 32-bit libraries well. Retro Catalog named it best budget handheld overall, and Retro Dodo compares the feel to a deck of cards.

  • Screen: 3.2" 4:3 IPS, 1024×768
  • Battery: 3,000mAh
  • OS: Linux (simple stock OS, custom firmware available)
  • Plays well: Game Boy through PS1; some N64 and Dreamcast
Pros
  • Sharp, bright screen for its size
  • Swappable shoulder buttons in the box
  • Easy stock OS and good community support
Cons
  • No analog sticks (D-pad can mimic one)
  • Small screen and no video out
  • Can run warm in heavy use

Best for: A sleek, pocket-sized handheld that just works

#3

Anbernic RG35XXSPBest Clamshell

Around $65 · $50–$100 tier

If you want the fold-up shape, this is the easy buy. GamesRadar calls it the best clamshell, and it can play more than GBA games, including PS1 and some Dreamcast.

  • Screen: 3.5" IPS, 640×480

Pros

Cons

#4

Analogue PocketBest for Real Cartridges

Around $220–$240 · $200+ tier

Not an emulation handheld in the usual sense, which makes it a distinctive gift for a collector. Reviewers rate it among the finest handhelds for original carts. Stock is inconsistent, so check who is selling it on Amazon.

  • Screen: 3.5" LCD, 615 PPI
  • Tech: FPGA hardware recreation, not software emulation
  • Plays: Original Game Boy, Game Boy Color and Game Boy Advance cartridges; adapters add Game Gear, Lynx and Neo Geo Pocket
Pros
  • Superb build quality and a stunning screen
  • Plays real cartridges with high accuracy
  • Large community of extra cores
Cons
  • High price
  • Often sold out, so verify the seller before buying

Best for: Game Boy collectors who want to use their real cartridges
