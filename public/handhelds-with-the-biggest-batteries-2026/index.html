Capacity is not battery life

A bigger number in mAh means a bigger battery, but how long it lasts depends on the screen, brightness, chip and the games you play. Use this as a starting point, and check reviews for real runtime. Frequently asked questions

Which handheld has the biggest battery?

Several share 8,000mAh, including the AYN Odin 2 Portal, AYN Odin 3, AYANEO Pocket S2, AYANEO Pocket DS and Mangmi Pocket Max.

Does a bigger battery always last longer?

No. Screen size, refresh rate and chip all change how fast it drains.

What does the Retroid Pocket 6 have?

A 6,000mAh battery in a lighter 304g body.
