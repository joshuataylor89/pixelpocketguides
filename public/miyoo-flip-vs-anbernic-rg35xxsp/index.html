Miyoo Flip vs Anbernic RG35XXSP: Which Flip Handheld Should You Buy?

Updated November 11, 2026

The Miyoo Flip and the Anbernic RG35XXSP are two of the most popular flip handhelds, both shaped like the Game Boy Advance SP and priced under $100. They look alike, but they are better at different things. Here is how they compare, and which one to buy.

Short answer: buy the Anbernic RG35XXSP if you want the safe, well-reviewed pick for GBA, SNES and PS1 games with HDMI out, and the Miyoo Flip if you want the extra power for N64 and Dreamcast games.

Quick answers

Is the Miyoo Flip better than the RG35XXSP?

It depends on the games. The Miyoo Flip is listed as best for Dreamcast and N64, and the RG35XXSP is the safer pick for GBA, SNES and PS1.

Which is cheaper, the Miyoo Flip or the RG35XXSP?

The RG35XXSP, at about $65 compared with about $78 for the Miyoo Flip. Prices change, so check Amazon.

Do both fold shut like the Game Boy Advance SP?

Yes. Both are flip (clamshell) handhelds with a hinged lid that protects the screen.

Do I need a microSD card?

Yes. Both use microSD cards for games and saves.
