Nintendo Switch 2 vs ROG Xbox Ally X vs Lenovo Legion Go 2

Updated November 11, 2026

The Nintendo Switch 2 and two Windows handhelds all arrived in 2025, and they cost very different amounts. Here is how they compare on paper.

Console or Windows handheld?

The Switch 2 is a console that plays Nintendo’s own games, and it costs less than half as much as the other two. The ROG Xbox Ally X and Legion Go 2 are Windows 11 PCs, so they can run PC game stores, emulators and other software. That freedom comes with a much higher price.

Who should buy which?

  • Switch 2: you mainly want Nintendo games and a lower price.
  • ROG Xbox Ally X: you want a Windows handheld that is lighter (715g) than the Legion Go 2.
  • Legion Go 2: you want the biggest and sharpest screen and have the budget for it.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Switch 2 run Windows or PC games?

No. It is a console with its own system software, not a Windows PC.

Which is the lightest?

The Switch 2 at 401g, then the ROG Xbox Ally X at 715g and the Legion Go 2 at 938g.

Which has the best screen?

The Legion Go 2, with an 8.8" 144Hz OLED panel.
