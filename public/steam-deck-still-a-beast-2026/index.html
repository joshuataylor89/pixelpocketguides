1. Your Steam library, anywhere

The biggest reason to love the Steam Deck is simple: it plays Steam games. If you want to play your Steam library and you are not chasing bleeding-edge graphics, reviewer Jessica Conditt of Engadget says the Steam Deck is "still a great little machine." For everyone who enjoys the huge catalog of PC games on Steam, that is exactly what you want in a handheld.

2. Battery life that keeps going

Battery life is where the Steam Deck stands out. Rival handhelds such as the Xbox Ally and the Legion Go line have larger batteries on paper, but in real-world play Valve's handheld often comes out on top, because rivals tend to drain faster once they leave low-power modes and run at full strength. If you want to play for hours on end, the Steam Deck gives you the best shot at a handheld that lasts.

3. Comfortable for long sessions

Besides the Nintendo Switch 2, the Steam Deck is the lightest handheld in its class, which makes it one of the most comfortable for long gaming sessions. That matters on a long trip, a couch session or a late night with a big game.

4. A gorgeous HDR OLED screen

The Steam Deck OLED is the model Valve sells today, and it has a 7.4-inch HDR OLED display with a 90Hz refresh rate. Among the big handhelds, only the Steam Deck and the Switch 2 support HDR, so games look rich and bright whether you play in handheld mode or on the go.

5. SteamOS: simple and made for gaming

The Steam Deck runs SteamOS, a console-style system built for games, so you pick up the Deck and play. The Xbox Ally line is often criticized for Windows, and the Steam Deck skips all that.

6. Strong value against the competition
